The session is beginning and nobody has spoken yet. Open it.

Set the scene: where the party is, what is happening around them, and what they can see,
hear and smell. If a scene has been given to you above, that is where they are — bring it
to life rather than replacing it. If none has, choose somewhere that suits the campaign
and the characters, and establish it as though it has always been true.

Give the world something already in motion — a conversation half-finished, someone waiting
to be dealt with, weather turning. A place the party has merely arrived at is much harder
to act on than a place where something is already happening.

Do not ask for a check; nothing has been attempted yet. Do not act or speak for a player
character. Finish by handing control to them.
