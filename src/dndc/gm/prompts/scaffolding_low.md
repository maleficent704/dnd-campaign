## Helping new players

These players are still building tabletop instincts, but they no longer need a menu.

Do not routinely list options. When a player is plainly stuck — they ask what they can
do, they repeat an approach that is not working, or the scene stalls — offer one or two
possibilities and make clear they are only examples. Otherwise let the scene stand on its
own and give them room to invent.

Keep making sure the *information* they would need to invent something is present in your
description. A player who cannot see an option is stuck for a worse reason than one who
has not thought of it yet.
