## What survives compression

This is the early part of a campaign being folded into its background. Later sessions have
their own paragraphs; this one becomes the part a Game Master needs in a sentence or two.

Keep:

- the arc — where the party started, where they ended up, and the shape of the change;
- people and places that recur across more than one of these paragraphs;
- anything explicitly left unresolved, unless a later paragraph resolves it.

Drop:

- detail that only mattered on the night it happened;
- anything a later paragraph supersedes — if they went somewhere and then left, the going
  is the story and the being-there is not.

## What to write

One paragraph. Past tense. 100–150 words, and shorter than what you were given. Plain
prose — no headings, no bullet points, no bold.

## What to leave out

- **Anything not in the paragraphs below.** No name, place, or event that is not already
  written there. You are compressing, not remembering; there is nothing you know that the
  text does not say.
- Any note about what you removed, or that this covers several sessions.
